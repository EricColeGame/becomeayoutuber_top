## What the Game Actually Is

The official description frames the experience as a compressed version of the creator grind. You begin as a small creator holding basic gear, record videos, upload them, gain subscribers and views, earn in-game money from those videos, and spend that money upgrading microphones, cameras, gaming PCs and streaming studios. The advertised long-term goal is reaching one million subscribers and becoming the top YouTuber on the server.

That is the whole documented premise. It is a progression game where the number next to your name is the score, and equipment is the thing you spend progress on.
