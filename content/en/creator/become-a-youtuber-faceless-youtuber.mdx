## Step-by-Step Production Workflow

Successful channels function like lean media newsrooms. Rather than improvising every video, top creators follow an assembly-line production pipeline where every step is standardized.

1. **Ideation and Title Testing:** Brainstorm topics based on verified search volume and audience interest before writing a single sentence.
2. **Hook and Script Drafting:** Structure your script so the opening thirty seconds directly address the title and thumbnail promise.
3. **Audio Production:** Record your voiceover with deliberate cadence and pause markers to ease video pacing.
4. **B-Roll and Asset Gathering:** Collect footage, animations, sound effects (SFX), and typography assets aligned with the narrative.
5. **Timeline Assembly:** Sync visual cuts to audio cues, keeping scene transitions dynamic to prevent viewer drop-off.
6. **Sound Design:** Layer background music at subtle volumes (-22 dB to -26 dB) and accent visual transitions with tactical sound effects.
