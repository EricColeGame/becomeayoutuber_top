If you searched for "become a Youtuber" and landed here, the important thing to understand up front is what this game is and what it is not: it is a Roblox experience about climbing an in-game creator ladder, not the Steam and console game *Youtubers Life*, not the separate Roblox experience *Be a YouTuber!*, and not a real-world course on launching a YouTube channel.

## The Advertised Game Loop

According to the official description, you begin as a small creator with basic gear. From there the described loop is straightforward:

- Record videos.
- Gain subscribers and views.
- Earn in-game money from those videos.
- Upload content.
- Spend that money on upgrades.
- Aim to become the top YouTuber on the server, with an advertised goal of one million subscribers.

That is the entire loop as the developer describes it. Notice how circular it is: recording feeds subscribers and views, subscribers and views feed money, and money feeds better gear that presumably improves what you can record. The description does not state how any of those conversions are calculated — no income formulas, no view-to-subscriber ratios, no time gates. Anyone who tells you the exact numbers is either reading them out of the live game or guessing.

## Playing Inside a 50-Player Server

The server size of up to 50 players matters more than it first appears, because the advertised ambition is to become "the top YouTuber on the server." That phrasing is server-scoped, not a global leaderboard claim. In practice, that means your competition is whoever else is in your session, and the ranking you are chasing resets with each new server you join. It also means the game is designed to be social: other players are visible competitors working the same loop.

Whether that competition involves direct interaction, leaderboard placement, or something else is not described on the official page. Check the in-game interface for a rankings display if that is what you care about.

Start by completing whatever tutorial or prompt sequence the game gives you, since the control scheme is not documented publicly and the in-game instructions are the authoritative source. ## FAQ

**Is this the same as Youtubers Life?**
No. *Youtubers Life* is a separate commercial game on Steam and consoles. This is a Roblox experience by Majicos_official.

**Are there codes for free cash or items?**
None are documented. The official description mentions no code system, no redemption box, and no free-cash codes. Verify any claim on the official page or the creator's community before believing it.

**How many players fit in a server?**
Up to 50, according to the official Roblox record.

**Does this game teach real YouTube growth?**
No. It is a simulation with its own in-game subscribers, views, and money. Real-world channel strategy is a different subject entirely.

**How do I reach one million subscribers?**
The goal is advertised; the path is not documented. Experiment in-game, check current prices and mechanics yourself, and treat third-party routes as suggestions rather than instructions.
