## The Core Loop: Record, Upload, Earn

The official description lays out a loop in plain terms:

1. Start as a small creator with basic gear.
2. Record videos.
3. Upload content.
4. Gain subscribers and views.
5. Earn in-game money from those videos.
6. Spend that money on upgrades.
7. Repeat until you reach the advertised one-million-subscriber goal.

That's the shape of the game as the developer presents it. What the description does **not** explain is the math behind it — how many views equal how many subscribers, how much a video pays out, whether watch time matters, or whether there are cooldowns between uploads. None of those numbers appear in the official material, so treat any specific figure you see elsewhere as an unverified claim rather than developer documentation.

The practical takeaway is simple: the game rewards repetition. Recording and uploading is the engine; everything else is a support system for it.
