## The Advertised Loop: Record, Upload, Earn, Grow

The description lists the actions in a sequence: record videos, gain subscribers and views, earn money from videos, upload content, then upgrade. Read it as a loop rather than a checklist, because each part feeds the next. Recording and uploading are described as separate steps, which suggests the game distinguishes capturing footage from publishing it — but the page does not document the exact controls or timing, so treat that as an inference until you see the interface yourself.

The important practical takeaway is that money is not the goal; it is the fuel. Views and subscribers are the score, and money is what converts that score into better equipment. New players who chase cash alone and ignore the subscriber count tend to stall, because the equipment that pushes growth is bought with the income that growth generates.
